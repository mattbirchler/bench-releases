Bench privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Bench is made by Matt Birchler (Birchtree Productions). This policy covers the Bench Mac app and the Bench iPhone app.

The short version

Bench doesn't collect, store, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or trackers.

The Mac app

Bench runs on your Mac and keeps its settings and project list there. It doesn't send your code, prompts, or anything else to us.

On its own, Bench only connects to the internet to check for updates. It downloads a small feed file from GitHub, where Bench is hosted. Like any website, GitHub may log that request.

Claude Code, which you install separately, talks to Anthropic under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. Bench doesn't change what Claude Code sends.

The iPhone app

The iPhone app connects directly to Bench on your own Mac, over your own Tailscale network. Your projects, sessions, and code go from your Mac to your phone and nowhere else. We never see them.

  • Camera: used only to scan the pairing code Bench shows on your Mac. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Pairing details: stored in your phone's keychain.
  • Notifications: off unless you turn them on. If you do, your phone's notification token is sent to your own Mac, which sends notifications through Apple's push service.

Children

Bench isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
